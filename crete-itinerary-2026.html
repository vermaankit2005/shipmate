Day by day

A proper timeline, not a checklist.

Times are suggestions. The order is deliberate: storm-safe first, then better-weather nature and beach days, with Balos protected for the calmest window.

Weather logic · updated 4 Oct
Sunday remains a storm-safety day: Crete is still under Red Code and gorge/trail access is restricted. Monday improves sharply. Tuesday–Saturday are the valuable outdoor window, with Thursday–Saturday reserved for Elafonissi, Falassarna and Balos.

Mon · 5 Oct

Chania Old Town + Venetian Harbour #3 experience

The perfect transition day: much better weather, but still flexible enough for any leftover morning shower.

ImprovingCity~20–30 min driveStroller-friendly
10:00

Leave Agia Marina and park at Talos Square

Park on the west side of the Old Town so you avoid the historic lanes and start immediately in the prettiest quarter.

10:30–11:30

Venetian Harbour promenade

Walk the waterfront slowly. If the sea is rough, enjoy the lighthouse from the harbour rather than walking far out on the breakwater.

11:30–12:45

Topanas + Old Town lanes

Venetian façades, Ottoman details, boutique shops and small courtyards. This is the part worth wandering without a strict route.

13:00–14:30

Lunch: Tamam

49 Spyridonos Zambeliou. Strong, reliable Old Town choice; highchairs are available. If there is a queue, eat at another side-street taverna rather than waiting too long with Prisha.

14:30–16:00

Gelato + harbour loop, then stop

Do not turn the day into a museum marathon. A café, dessert and slow waterfront loop is enough.

🅿 ParkingTalos Square parking is the first choice. It keeps you outside the pedestrian core and is easy with a stroller.
👶 With PrishaUse the stroller along the waterfront. Cobbled inner lanes are manageable slowly; carrier is optional, not required.
🅿 Talos Square🍽 Tamam
Tue · 6 Oct

Botanical Park + Theriso Gorge #5 experience

First genuinely strong nature day after the storm. Do the park in the morning, eat there, then use Theriso as a scenic drive rather than a hike.

~24°C · dryNature~40–45 min to parkCarrier useful
09:15

Leave Agia Marina

Aim to be at the Botanical Park around 10:00. Bring water and proper shoes.

10:00–12:00

Walk the main Botanical Park route

Fruit trees, herbs, Mediterranean plants and valley views. Paths are uneven and can remain damp after the storm.

12:00–13:15

Lunch at the Botanical Park restaurant

This keeps the day efficient. The restaurant serves Cretan food on-site, so there is no reason to detour for lunch.

13:30–14:15

Drive toward Theriso

Use the gorge road only after confirming restrictions are lifted and the route is open. The drive itself is the attraction.

14:15–15:30

Theriso village + coffee / early bite

Walk the small village center. If you want a second food stop, Limeri is the strongest-rated local taverna option.

15:30

Head back

No extra mountain road. Keep this as a half-to-three-quarter day.

🅿 ParkingBotanical Park has on-site parking. In Theriso, use legal roadside / village-center spaces and avoid blocking narrow lanes.
⚠️ Check firstStorm restrictions covered gorges and nature trails through Sunday. Confirm the Theriso road is fully open Tuesday morning before committing.
🅿 Botanical Park📍 Theriso🍽 Limeri
Wed · 7 Oct

Lake Kournas + Georgioupolis #6 experience

A gentle family nature day between the mountain outing and the three premium coastal days.

Mostly sunnyEasy~1h05–1h20 drive
09:30

Drive to Lake Kournas

Use the main road east. Do not rush; this is the relaxed day.

10:45–12:00

Lakeside walk

Stay around the accessible shore. You do not need to circle the whole lake.

12:00–12:45

Optional pedal boat

Only in calm conditions and only if you are happy with the child life-jacket fit. Otherwise skip it without losing much.

13:00–14:15

Lunch: Georgia's Traditional Taverna

Highly rated family-style cooking; family reviews specifically mention toddlers. Parking is available across the road.

14:30–16:00

Georgioupolis

About 15 minutes away. Park centrally, walk the seafront, have coffee or ice cream and view the Agios Nikolaos chapel from shore. Do not take Prisha onto the rocky causeway if it is wet or wavy.

🅿 ParkingAt Kournas, use the customer/roadside lots by the lakeside businesses. In Georgioupolis, use central public parking near the square/harbour.
🎒 BringLight jacket, water, stroller, and a change of clothes if Prisha goes near the water.
🅿 Lake Kournas🍽 Georgia's🅿 Georgioupolis
Thu · 8 Oct

Elafonissi #2 experience

Give the first premium beach slot to Elafonissi: shallow turquoise water, pink-tinged sand and the best toddler-friendly lagoon experience of the trip.

Sunny · ~25°CBeach~1h35–1h50 each way
08:30

Leave Agia Marina early

The road is scenic but slow. Keep this as the only major destination today.

10:15

Park and walk to the lagoon

Use the main signed parking area before the protected beach zone. Take the carrier if you want one; a stroller is not useful once you reach sand.

10:30–14:00

Lagoon + beach time

Walk a little beyond the first crowded section. The shallow water is the experience; there is no need to chase a beach club.

14:15–15:30

Lunch: Glykeria

About 4.5 km from Elafonissi, with strong 2026 family reviews, highchairs and free off-street parking. This is a much better meal than relying on beach kiosks.

15:30

Drive back

Optional 15-minute coffee stop around Elos only if Prisha is happy. Otherwise go straight back.

🅿 ParkingFollow signs for the main Elafonissi parking areas and expect a walk to the sand. Do not try to drive closer into protected areas.
🎒 BringWater, snacks, beach shoes, sun protection, towel, light layer, Prisha's food/water bottle and carrier.
🌬 What mattersWind matters more than temperature. If Thursday suddenly becomes windy, swap Elafonissi with another dry day.
🍽 FoodGlykeria is open through the day and has its own parking; ideal after the beach before the long return drive.
🅿 Elafonissi parking🍽 Glykeria

Falassarna + sunset #4 experience

A wide, dramatic west-coast beach with a much easier road than Elafonissi. Make sunset part of the plan instead of wasting time on a dedicated Kissamos town stop.

Sunny · ~26°CBeach~55–65 min driveSunset day
10:30

Leave Agia Marina

Later start is intentional because sunset is worth staying for.

11:30–14:00

Pachia Ammos — main Falassarna beach

Stay on the broad main sandy section. No need to drive around looking for a “secret” cove.

14:15–15:45

Lunch: Spilios Taverna

Traditional Cretan food, panoramic sea view, free parking and a playground — particularly useful with Prisha.

16:00–18:40

Back to the beach / slow afternoon

Swim, play in the sand, or simply relax. Keep energy for sunset rather than adding Kissamos.

~18:50

Falassarna sunset

Watch from the beach or Spilios terrace. If Prisha is tired, sunset from the taverna is the easiest version.

🅿 ParkingUse the main Pachia Ammos beach parking / beach-business lots. Spilios has free off-street parking.
⚠️ Wind ruleFalassarna is exposed. If west/northwest wind becomes strong, swap the day rather than forcing a miserable beach.

Sat · 10 Oct

Balos Lagoon + Gramvousa boat #1 experience

The biggest “wow” day of the trip. Protect it for the calmest forecast and do it by boat from Kissamos — not by rental car on the rough Balos road.

Best protected dayBoat~40–50 min to portWeather-dependent
Fri evening

Confirm the ferry + wind

Book/confirm the Balos + Gramvousa sailing. The official operator runs through 31 Oct 2026, but the exact departure can vary. Be at Kissamos Port 40 minutes before departure.

Departure − 1h30

Leave Agia Marina

This gives you driving time, parking and the required 40-minute pre-boarding buffer without stress.

Port

Park free at Kissamos Port

The official cruise operator confirms free parking at the port. Use the port ticket office directly in front of the vessels.

Boat day

Gramvousa + Balos

Let the ferry timetable dictate the day. Gramvousa has the dramatic fortress/island stop; Balos is the lagoon highlight. Do not attempt the steep Gramvousa fortress climb with Prisha unless conditions and energy are excellent.

On board

Food / practical

There are no shops at Gramvousa or Balos. The ship has a self-service restaurant-bar. Official guidance says onboard payments are by card or instant payment — not cash.

Return

Eat in Kissamos only if everyone still has energy

Otherwise drive straight back. This day is already complete; do not add another attraction.

💶 2026 fareBalos + Gramvousa: €40 per passenger age 13+, €24 age 3–12. Children up to 2 travel free. A €1 municipal fee applies to passengers over 13 visiting Balos.
🎒 BringWater, snacks, hats, sunscreen, beach shoes, comfortable shoes, towels, light jacket, Prisha's food/water and carrier.
🅿 ParkingFree parking at Kissamos Port is confirmed by the official operator.
🚫 Do notDo not take the rental car down the rough Balos access road. Boat is the family-friendly choice.
🅿 Kissamos Port🎟 Cruise booking
